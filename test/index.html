About Me

My name is Alex and I am a passionate amateur photographer based in southern Sweden. I specialize in landscape and wildlife photography, capturing the beauty of nature in all its forms. I might have a weak spot for waterfalls, clouds and mountains, althought there's not much of those around here.

Currrent equipment includes a Sony A7R IV and a variety of lenses for different shooting scenarios. But I also have a Fuji GFX 100s with a wide-angle lens for those situations where I need to capture the grandeur of the landscape.

As with many other photographers, I tend to have too much equipment such as backpacks, tripods, filters, since a lot of gear makes better photos, ...right?.
